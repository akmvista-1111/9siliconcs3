## Step 1: Identify the Big Problem
### Main Problem
Students are taking a lot of time to buy their food, which can make students late for the next class.
---
## Step 2: Identify the Sub-Problems
1. Some students take too long to decide what to order.
2. The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
3. There is no system to track which food items are running out.
4. Students have to wait in long lines to order.
---
## Step 3: Apply Computational Thinking Skills
| Sub-Problem | CT Skill | Proposed Solution |
|---|---|---|
| Some students take too long to decide what to order. | Abstraction | Display the available food items and each of their prices. |
| The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change. | Algorithm | Use a system that will automatically calculate the total and change. |
| There is no system to track which food items are running out. | Pattern Recognition | Track the remaining quantities of food items to determine the food items that are running out. |
| Students have to wait in long lines to order. | Decomposition | Divide the process of ordering into simple steps to make the queue move faster. |
---
## Step 4: Algorithmic Solution
### Selected Sub-Problem
The cashier has to manually calculate totals and give change.
### Pseudocode
```text
START
Display the food items and each of their prices
Student select food items
Calculate the total price
Input the money given
Calculate the change
```
